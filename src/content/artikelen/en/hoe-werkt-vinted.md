You want a jumper for less than the shop price, or you want to sell clothes you no longer wear. Vinted looks simple until you place your first order. Then there are deadlines, fees and buttons nobody points out.

I have made more than 2,500 transactions on Vinted. This article is about the platform's rules and not about my own figures.

The explanation follows Vinted's official pages and an article by Test-Aankoop as they stood online on 31 October 2026. Vinted can change rules and amounts.

## Vinted is an intermediary and not a shop

Vinted is a platform where users buy, sell or swap items. According to the terms of use, Vinted itself is not a party to the transaction between two users. You buy from another member and not from Vinted.

To register you must be 18. Minors need a parent or guardian to supervise them. A seller describes the item accurately, mentions defects, sets a price and adds at least one good-quality photo.

## How to sell an item

Listing an item is free and you can list as many as you like. Vinted screens every item automatically against the catalogue rules. That usually takes up to a minute but can sometimes take three or four days.

Once your item is sold you have five working days to ship it. A longer period is only possible if the buyer agrees. If you do not ship in time, the order is cancelled automatically and the buyer gets a refund.

You ship with the shipping option the buyer chose and track the parcel through the chat with the buyer. Vinted recommends photographing the item and the packed parcel before you send it. Take photos of the inside and the outside, because they help with problems in transit.

**My advice:** take those photos for every parcel, even for an item worth a few euros. You do not know in advance which parcel will run into trouble.

## How to buy an item

You find an item and save it to your favourites. Then you ask the seller a question or make an offer. Check the measurements and look at the seller's reviews.

If the seller has more items you like, you can buy a bundle. At checkout you enter your address and choose a payment method and a delivery option. The Vinted fee is added to the total price automatically.

When Vinted tells you the parcel has been delivered, you approve the order or report a problem. With a problem you first try to find a solution together with the seller. If that fails, Vinted can investigate and refund you where appropriate.

You have two days from Vinted's delivery notification to do so. If you do nothing, Test-Aankoop writes that the order is closed automatically and you can no longer get a refund through Vinted.

**My advice:** unpack your parcel within those two days and check it straight away.

## What it costs

Selling is free according to the Help Centre. Paid options such as Boosts and Spotlights are optional and get your item seen faster.

The buyer pays the Vinted fee. The Help Centre still calls it the Buyer Protection fee. According to the terms on vinted.nl it is €0.70 plus 5 percent of the item price, excluding shipping and VAT.

Test-Aankoop gives the same amount in an article of 13 March 2026. The English Help Centre page says the amount can differ depending on the item, the order value or a bundle. So check the amount the app shows at checkout.

Vinted says the fee covers things like security, payment tools, refunds for lost or damaged items and customer service. What that means for your selling price is explained in [the hidden costs of Vinted](/en/articles/hidden-costs-of-vinted).

## How and when you get paid

Before your first sale you activate your Wallet. That is where the money from your sales ends up.

An order is complete once the buyer approves it or the two days pass without a report. After that the amount becomes available in your Wallet within two days.

With that balance you can shop on Vinted or transfer it to your bank account. A payout to your bank can take up to five working days.

## What Vinted covers when something goes wrong

If you pay through Vinted, you get a refund in three cases according to Vinted. The item does not arrive, arrives damaged or clearly does not match the description. You report that within two days.

Sending an item back is not a right. According to Test-Aankoop the legal 14-day cooling-off period only applies to a professional seller and not to a private person.

You report a problem in the order itself. How that works is covered in [your parcel is lost or damaged on Vinted](/en/articles/vinted-package-lost-or-damaged). Which routes Vinted names for contacting support is covered in [how to contact Vinted support](/en/articles/contact-vinted-support).

## Stay in the app and watch for scams

Vinted asks you to keep chatting in the app and not to share personal details or conversations through other apps or SMS.

Test-Aankoop describes how scammers sometimes move the conversation to email or WhatsApp. Be careful if a buyer offers more than your asking price and sends you a payment link to check that you are trustworthy. Avoid foreign cheques and payments through Western Union.

Also look at the other person's profile. According to Test-Aankoop a profile without photos, a description or reviews is suspicious. Report scams to Vinted as soon as possible.

## In one sentence

On Vinted you list for free, ship within five working days and get your money after the buyer approves or two days pass. The buyer pays the Vinted fee and has two days to report a problem.

> Stay in the app and report a problem within two days.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Vinted free?

Selling is free. You list items at no cost and the Help Centre names no selling fees, apart from optional extras such as Boosts. As a buyer you pay €0.70 plus 5 percent of the item price as the Vinted fee, plus shipping.

### Who pays the Vinted fee?

The buyer. It is added to the total price automatically at checkout. So you see it before you pay.

### How long do I have to ship a parcel?

Five working days after the purchase. A longer period is only possible if the buyer agrees. Otherwise the order is cancelled and the buyer gets a refund.

### When do I get my money?

Once the order is complete the amount becomes available in your Wallet within two days. A transfer to your bank can take up to five working days.

### Can I send an item back?

Not just like that. According to Test-Aankoop the 14-day cooling-off period does not apply to private sellers. If the item does not match the description or arrived damaged or not at all, report it within two days.
