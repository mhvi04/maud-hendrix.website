You sell an item and the questions that follow are practical. Which carrier do I use, who pays for shipping and by when does the parcel have to leave? On Vinted, the buyer chooses the carrier and pays the shipping costs.

For you as a seller, the deadline is what matters. Miss it and the sale disappears. The rules below come from Vinted's help pages and apply as of 4 October 2026.

## The buyer chooses the carrier and pays for shipping

As soon as a buyer purchases the item, the buyer picks the carrier you have to use. Shipping costs are always paid by the buyer and are added to the order.

The amount depends on the size of the parcel and on the location of the buyer and the seller. The help page gives no example amounts, so I do not give any either.

The page I read says nothing about free shipping or about shipping costs on a bundle. I therefore cannot confirm what you see in the app on those points.

## Vinted provides the label

After the order, Vinted sends you a prepaid shipping label. You receive it by email and in your Vinted inbox.

The buyer pays the shipping costs, so you pay nothing for the label itself. How you hand the parcel in afterwards is not stated in the source I read, so I do not write it down.

## You have five working days to ship

After the order you have five working days to ship the item. Vinted did not change that in the new terms from 5 October 2026 — see [what changes in the new Vinted terms](/en/articles/wat-verandert-er-in-de-nieuwe-vinted-voorwaarden) —.

If the deadline passes, the order is cancelled automatically. Five working days is not a guideline. After that, the sale stops.

## Need more time

If you cannot make the deadline, you can request an extension of three or five working days. That goes through the buyer, who has to agree. The buyer may also refuse.

So ask for an extension early and politely. I deliberately do not say how often buyers agree, because I have no figures on that.

## Using a different carrier

If you ship with a different carrier than the one the buyer chose, Vinted cannot track the parcel. In that case Vinted has to cancel the transaction and refund the buyer.

Use the label and the carrier from the order. If something still goes wrong in transit, the buyer has only two days after the delivery notice to react — see [your parcel is lost or damaged on Vinted](/en/articles/pakketje-kwijt-of-beschadigd-op-vinted) —.

## How long delivery takes for the buyer

On its Belgian help page, Vinted writes that a shipment within the same country usually takes between two and five working days. International shipments usually take between four and seven working days.

In that estimate, Saturdays, Sundays and the main national public holidays usually do not count. These are Vinted averages and not a guarantee.

## How I ship myself

I try to ship every parcel within 48 hours. That is my own target and it sits well inside the five working days Vinted gives.

I always keep the buyer informed. I take a photo of the packed parcel and message the buyer when it goes to the post. The photo helps build the buyer's trust and serves as proof if something goes wrong.

I put quite some time into packing and use a neat shipping bag. You can buy one for a few cents on Chinese webshops.

In my sales export up to March 2026, 33 orders were cancelled by Vinted because they were not shipped on time. That is about 2.5 percent of my sales. I cannot get my average shipping speed from that export, because it contains no shipping date.

## The shortest summary

The buyer chooses the carrier and pays the shipping costs. You get a prepaid label from Vinted and have five working days to ship. An extension of three or five working days is possible if the buyer agrees. After that, the order is cancelled automatically.

> Ship within five working days and with the carrier from the order.
